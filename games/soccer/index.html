Soccer Pool — Dad vs The Kids

♥ Happy Father's Day! Today you play soccer pool against your kids. ♥

Each side has 5 pieces: goalkeeper, two defenders, defensive midfielder, striker.

Hold & drag back from a piece to pull it. Release to flick — the further you pull, the harder the hit.

You control both sides — flick your own pieces, then pass the mouse and let someone else flick theirs.

Score in your own attacking net for a point: kids attack the left net, dad attacks the right net.

First to 5 wins! No turn limit — play as long as you like.
